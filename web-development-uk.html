A development partner that already knows the UK market

Most of our recent work has been for British businesses, so we are used to what UK clients expect: realistic quotes, plain-English updates, and websites that are written for UK customers rather than translated for them. That means en-GB spelling, prices in pounds, VAT shown the way UK buyers expect and contact details that meet Companies Act rules.

Our UK projects lean towards commercial vehicles and logistics. We built the stock marketplace and admin system for Midland Commercials, the public website for BRIT Commercials, and an operations platform for a haulage firm covering jobs, MOT and tax reminders, defect-to-repair tracking and daily walkaround checks from a driver app.

If you are a UK startup, agency or established business, we can take a project from first brief to launch and then look after it with a monthly maintenance plan.

Local requirements

Built for UK rules from day one

The details that make a site feel local and keep you on the right side of UK rules.

UK GDPR and the DPA 2018

Privacy notices, a lawful basis for every form, data-retention rules and a data processing agreement when we handle personal data for you.

PECR cookie consent

Analytics and marketing cookies only load after opt-in, with a clear consent banner instead of a pre-ticked one.

Companies Act disclosures

Your company name, registered number, registered office and VAT number placed where the law expects them on your site and emails.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Keyboard navigation, contrast and screen-reader labels, which helps you meet Equality Act duties and improves SEO.

Pounds and VAT done properly

GBP pricing, VAT-inclusive prices for consumers and VAT-exclusive pricing for trade customers where you need both.

UK data hosting

We can deploy to AWS London (eu-west-2) or your own UK host so customer data stays in the UK.
