Transport Management System · UK haulier

Most small and mid-sized hauliers run on a mix of spreadsheets, paper check sheets, WhatsApp groups and a calendar full of MOT dates. It works until the fleet grows, a defect slips through or DVSA asks for records that take days to pull together.

We build transport management systems that bring jobs, vehicles, drivers and compliance into one place. Drivers do their walkaround checks and report defects in a mobile app, and the office sees every check, defect and due date on a single dashboard, with a clear trail from each reported defect to its repair.

This is not theory. We designed and built a TMS and driver app for a UK haulage business, and it is in daily use. Our guides to TMS for UK hauliers and digital walkaround checks explain what to look for. See our custom walkaround check app development service for the driver workflow.

Features

What your TMS does

Built around the way UK operators work, and the records DVSA expects you to keep.

Jobs and dispatch

Create, plan and assign jobs to vehicles and drivers, with live status from the road and locations on Google Maps.

Walkaround checks and defects

Daily checks in the driver app with photo defect reports, and instant alerts to the office when something fails.

Vehicle compliance reminders

MOT, road tax, insurance and inspection due dates tracked for every vehicle, with reminders before anything lapses.

Driver documents

Licence, Driver CPC, medical and tachograph card expiry dates in one place, with alerts for the office and the driver.

A driver app drivers will use

A simple React Native app for iOS and Android with large controls, offline support and push alerts for new jobs.

Audit-ready records

Defect-to-repair history, checks and reminders pulled into reports for any vehicle and date range in a few clicks.
