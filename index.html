Marketplace · United Kingdom

Midland Commercials: a truck marketplace that sells around the clock

Problem
Trade buyers compare trucks online long before they call, so the dealer's website had to do the work of a salesperson, at any hour.
What we built
A React and Node.js marketplace with live stock and filters, an AI sales assistant, a part-exchange enquiry flow and an admin dashboard.
Result
Live at midlandcommercials.com. The dealer's team runs stock and enquiries on their own, and every vehicle has a page built to be found on Google.

Accukount Pharma: pharmacy stock counts done on a phone

Problem
Stock counts on paper and spreadsheets were slow, error-prone and hard to compare against the pharmacy's own records.
What we built
A platform where staff scan and count on a smartphone, counts reconcile automatically and discrepancies are flagged for managers.
Result
Live for Canadian pharmacies, with web dashboards plus iOS and Android apps in English and French.
