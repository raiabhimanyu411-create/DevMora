Frequently Asked Questions About Web Development

Web development costs vary based on project complexity. A basic website starts from ₹50,000, while complex applications can range from ₹2-10 lakhs. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your requirements.

How long does it take to develop a website?

Timeline depends on project scope. A simple website takes 2-4 weeks, while complex web applications can take 2-4 months. We follow agile methodology with regular updates and milestones.

Yes, we handle complete deployment including domain registration, hosting setup on AWS or shared hosting, SSL certificates, and ongoing server maintenance.

Can you work with clients outside India?

Absolutely! We work with clients across UK, USA, Canada, and Australia. We maintain clear communication through email, video calls, and project management tools.

Do you provide ongoing support after project delivery?

Yes, we offer comprehensive support and maintenance packages. This includes bug fixes, security updates, feature additions, and technical support.
