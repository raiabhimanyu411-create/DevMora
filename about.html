Our journey

Key milestones

2021 · The beginning

Devmora Technology founded

Started as a freelance operation in Greater Noida, building custom React frontends for local businesses and startups. First client shipped in under 2 weeks.

2022 · First major build

Sellaids, multi-vendor e-commerce

Delivered a full multi-vendor e-commerce platform with 1000+ products, Cloudinary CDN, admin dashboards and vendor management panels built from scratch.

2023 · Enterprise systems

Enterprise data & booking portals

Delivered secure, large-scale data portals on AWS EC2 with Sequelize ORM, online payments, booking workflows and interactive map-based data catalogues for an energy-sector organisation.

2023 · International expansion

First UK client, BRIT Commercials

Began our international journey with a UK truck dealership: full SEO overhaul, GTmetrix Grade A performance and a web retainer that continues today.

2024 · Present

SaaS, transport & healthcare platforms

20+ projects delivered worldwide: Accukount and AssetKount stock-verification SaaS for Canada, Midland Commercials, a transport management system with a driver app, and a hospital management system. Currently accepting new projects.
